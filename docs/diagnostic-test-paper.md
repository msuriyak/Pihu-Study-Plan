## SECTION B — Physics, board style · 21 marks · 30 minutes

*Write full answers longhand, as in a board exam. Draw diagrams wherever applicable — marks are allotted for them.*

**B1.** (3 marks) State Coulomb's law and write it in vector form. How is the force between two charges affected if the free space between them is replaced by a medium of dielectric constant K?

**B2.** (3 marks) Define electric flux and give its SI unit. What is the net electric flux through a closed surface that encloses an electric dipole? Justify your answer.

**B3.** (3 marks) Draw the electric field lines for (i) a single isolated positive charge and (ii) two equal positive point charges placed near each other. State any one property of electric field lines.

**B4.** (5 marks) Using Gauss's theorem, derive an expression for the electric field due to an infinitely long straight uniformly charged wire of linear charge density λ, at a perpendicular distance r from it. Draw the Gaussian surface you use.

**B5.** (5 marks) Derive an expression for the torque acting on an electric dipole placed in a uniform external electric field. Hence define electric dipole moment. Under what condition is the torque maximum?
